How to Confirm You’re at the Right Location:

Take a photo that proves you are at the correct property. Please ensure the photo includes something identifiable, such as the:

  • House number
  • Mailbox
  • Street sign
  • Front of the property
Note: If the exact address isn’t visible, take a clear, wide shot of the location so the property can be easily recognized.

How to Take a Photo of Completed Work:

Take photos showing that the work is fully complete and the area is clean. Please be sure to include:

  • The finished work (from a few different angles)
  • The surrounding area (to show it has been thoroughly cleaned up)
Note: Make sure all photos are clear and show the full result of the finished work.
