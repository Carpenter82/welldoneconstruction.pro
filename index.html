Company Standards & Protocols ("The Books")

1. Trade Specialization

We hire specialists and generalists. Whether you are Framing, Electrical, Plumbing, or Finish, you can be expected to perform within many trade classes and grow. General Labor supports the trades; they do not replace them.

2. Tier Eligibility

Pay is project-based. Although we will try to match you with the best fit, be prepared to be dispatched in an array combining many trades. Tier 1 (Support) requires reliability. Tier 2 (Skilled) requires tool ownership and autonomy. Tier 3 (Lead) requires OSHA credentials.

3. Field Expectations

Zero tolerance for safety violations, substance use, or no-shows. All hours will be compensated for based on skill level for project only. Location data and visual proof of work must be logged via the Portal.
