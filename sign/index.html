Master Employment Agreement

1. Scope of Work

This agreement confirms that the undersigned is an At-Will Employee (W-2) of Well Done Construction LLC. The employee agrees to perform duties as assigned by the Dispatch system.

2. Safety Standards

The employee agrees to adhere to all safety policies and procedures communicated verbally or published online by Well Done Construction LLC. Noncompliance with any safety policy constitutes grounds for immediate termination of employment.

3. Compensation

Compensation is determined on a per-job basis and defined in the applicable job manifest. Pay rates are established according to the skill level required for the specific job. Workers designated at higher tiers may be assigned to perform work at any tier, at the rate specified for that job. Job assignments are made at the sole discretion of Well Done Construction LLC based on the worker’s qualifications and skill set. All payments are processed through the Company’s payroll service provider in use at the time.
